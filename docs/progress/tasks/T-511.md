# T-511 — Cross-machine dogfood and first release

The final gate. Every claim in the definition of done is verified on real machines with real harnesses before anything is published.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two developers on two machines with two different harnesses exchange messages over a week.
- [ ] Every bullet in the product requirements definition of done is verified and recorded, with evidence.
- [ ] Issues found during dogfooding are filed as tasks and either fixed or explicitly deferred with a reason.
- [ ] The first version is tagged and published.
- [ ] A release report records what worked, what broke, and what changed as a result.

## Notes

PRD section 55, Plan section 8 M5. Do not publish until every checklist item has been observed, not assumed.
